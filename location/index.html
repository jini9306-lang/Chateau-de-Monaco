Casino de Monte-Carlo

Visitor Info

Belle Époque gaming halls where fortunes have turned since 1863 — marble atriums, gilded ceilings, and the hush of the roulette wheel.

Hours

Public gaming rooms open daily from 2:00 PM.

Dress Code

Smart casual by day; jacket required in the private rooms after 8:00 PM.

Entry

Passport required. A small admission fee applies to the gaming rooms; the atrium and terraces are open to all.

Port Hercule

Harbor Guide

Monaco's natural deep-water harbour, ringed by superyachts and the cliffs of the Rock — the beating heart of the principality since Antiquity.

Best For

Golden-hour walks along the quay, yacht-watching, and the Café de Paris terrace just above.

Grand Prix Season

Each May, the harbour becomes the epicentre of the Monaco Grand Prix — grandstands rise along the water's edge.

Getting There

A ten-minute walk downhill from the Casino, or moor directly at the marina by boat.
